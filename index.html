working session · researchwhat was actually found

drafts and limits, not a finished learner

Concepts from the chapter

Semantic precision about 83–90%. After a cheap filter, purity 94–100%. Expert-index F1 stalls near 52–55%, and that ceiling is structural.

Profile is four different things

A wish, an observation, an estimate, and the next action. “Dislikes Socratic” may mean “in a hurry”.

Affect is a proposal

Confusion, frustration, uncertainty, from the dialogue only. No camera. It must not rewrite mastery, and it is not running on students.

Signals are candidates

Time on the material, coverage, reread, skip, time-to-first-help, codelab runs and test failures. Not one formula. Dwell alone is weak.
